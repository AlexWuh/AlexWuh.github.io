<div class="research-focuses">
  <section class="research-focus">
    <div class="research-focus__content">
      <h2>Integer Programming</h2>
      <p>My research develops scalable methodologies for formulating and solving large-scale mixed-integer optimization problems. I investigate language-model-assisted mathematical modeling, learning-augmented optimization for accelerating search and decision processes, GPU-native algorithmic primitives, and decomposition-and-coordination methods such as surrogate Lagrangian relaxation. The overarching goal is to build robust end-to-end optimization systems that combine modeling intelligence, algorithmic structure, and modern computing architectures.</p>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="research-focus">
    <div class="research-focus__content">
      <h2>Large Language Model Systems</h2>
      <p>My research explores systems methodologies for deploying and serving large language models across heterogeneous computing platforms. It spans hardware-aware model placement, inference-runtime acceleration, memory and communication optimization, and automated end-to-end deployment. I am particularly interested in closed-loop systems that continuously profile execution, diagnose bottlenecks, and refine deployment and inference policies through iterative self-improvement.</p>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="research-focus">
    <div class="research-focus__content">
      <h2>Power Market Trading</h2>
      <p>My research studies algorithmic intelligence across the full lifecycle of power-market trading, from probabilistic forecasting and strategy construction to execution, risk-aware decision-making, and ex-post performance attribution. I develop AI-assisted decision frameworks and autonomous trading agents that integrate market information with physical power-system constraints, with an emphasis on transparent, adaptive, and operationally reliable decisions.</p>
    </div>
  </section>
